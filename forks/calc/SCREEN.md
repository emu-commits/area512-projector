A formula starts with `=`:
`=A1+B2*3`  `=(A1-5)/2`  `=B3/4`
`+ - * / ( )`, numbers and cells (`B3`;
an empty cell is 0).
`=SUM(B1:B9)` `AVG` `MIN` `MAX` `COUNT`:
over a range; they skip empty and text.
`=B$1` `=$B$1`: `$` stays put when copied
(`c` `v`) or filled (`d` `r`).
`#ERR`: a slip, /0, text in a sum, a loop
To type text in a cell: Enter first.
